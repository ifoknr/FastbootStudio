## purpose
The app's version and credits, and its settings: the language, the appearance, the drawing mode and update checks. The welcome tour and What's new open from here too.

## steps
1. **Language**: pick one, and the app restarts in it. Arabic turns the whole window right to left.
2. **Appearance**: Dark, Light, or As Windows. It applies after a restart.
3. **Drawing**: if the window flickers, loses menu items or shows striped buttons (some graphics drivers and remote-desktop apps), choose **Software (steady)**.
4. **Updates**: leave **Check for updates automatically** on, or press **Check now**. A new version shows what changed and downloads only if you say so.
5. **Show the tour again** walks you through the app; **What's new** lists this version's changes.

## tips
- An update download is kept only when its SHA-256 matches the release, so a damaged or swapped file never stays.
- The app never replaces itself: close it and unzip the new version, the same way you installed it.
- Questions and ideas: the Telegram group, or IFOKNR's GitHub.

## terms
SHA-256 | A fingerprint of a file; a single changed byte gives a different one
Drawing | How the window is painted: by the graphics card, or by the processor (steadier)
