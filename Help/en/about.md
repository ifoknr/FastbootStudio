## purpose
The app's version, who made it and what it is built on, with the links to reach IFOKNR.

## steps
1. **Check for updates** looks for a new version right away; the answer shows in Settings.
2. The rows under IFOKNR open Telegram, the Telegram group, GitHub and all of IFOKNR's tools.
3. **Built on** credits Fastboot Enhance by LibXZR, with its project, thread and MIT license.

## tips
- The language, the appearance, the text size and updates are in **Settings**.
- Questions and ideas: the Telegram group, or IFOKNR's GitHub.
