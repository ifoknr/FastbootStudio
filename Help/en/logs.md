## purpose
Everything the app ran in this session, and every answer from fastboot and adb. It is what to send when you ask for help.

## steps
1. Do what went wrong once more, so it is in the log.
2. Press **Copy**, and paste it in your message to the group or in a GitHub issue.
3. **Clear** empties the log; it starts again empty with each start of the app.

## tips
- The log shows serial numbers of your phones. Remove them before posting in public if you prefer.
- If the app ever closes by itself, the error is also saved in a crash.log in your temporary folder (%TEMP%\FastbootStudio).

## terms
Log | The list of commands and answers, oldest at the top
Serial | Your phone's own ID as fastboot and adb show it
